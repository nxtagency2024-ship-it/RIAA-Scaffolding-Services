1. Introduction

RIAA Scaffolding LLC (“RIAA Scaffolding,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit https://riaascaffold.com (the “Website”) or submit an inquiry about our scaffolding services in California.

Our services include scaffolding rental, delivery and pickup, installation, and dismantling for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

Where consent is required by applicable law for a particular data-processing activity, we will obtain that consent as required.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request or contact us through the Website, we may collect information that you provide, including:

  • Name: To identify you and respond to your inquiry.
  • Email address: To communicate with you regarding your request.
  • Phone number: To contact you about your project.
  • ZIP code: To identify the general location of your project and assess service availability.
  • Project details: To understand your requested services and assess project requirements.
  • Project address: To understand the project location and evaluate relevant access and service requirements.

The exact information collected depends on the fields displayed on the form and the information you choose to submit.

Please provide accurate information and submit only information reasonably necessary for your inquiry. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the Website unless it is specifically required and an appropriate method has been provided.
