Questions

Before you ask

How fast can you quote?

You'll get a fixed quote within 24 hours of the site survey. Most surveys happen within two working days of your enquiry.

Is the survey free?

Yes. There's no charge and no obligation to go ahead.

Do I need a permit or licence?

If the scaffold sits on or over a public highway, a licence is usually needed. We can apply on your behalf.

How long can the scaffold stay up?

For as long as you need it. Hire is charged weekly, and extending is a phone call.

What areas do you cover?

Tell us your postcode in the quote form and we'll confirm.
