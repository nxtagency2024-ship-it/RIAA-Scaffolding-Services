Good to know

Questions, answered

Still have questions? Give us a call, and we'll be happy to assist.

How quickly can you put scaffolding up?

Most jobs are surveyed within a day or two and erected within 48 hours of accepting the quote. Emergency call-outs run 24/7.

Do I need a permit or licence?

If the scaffold sits on or over a public pavement or road, a licence is usually needed. We handle the application for you.

How long is the hire period?

As long as you need it. The quote states an initial hire period and extending is a quick phone call.

Are you insured and accredited?

Yes. We carry public liability insurance, are NASC members and all our scaffolders hold CISRS cards.

Who inspects the scaffold?

A competent person inspects it after erection and at least every seven days, with records kept on site.
