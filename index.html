Good to know

Questions, answered

Still have questions? Give us a call, and we'll be happy to assist.

How quickly can you put scaffolding up?

Timing depends on the size of the project. Contact us for a free estimate and we'll give you a schedule.

Do I need a permit?

If scaffolding is placed on or over a public sidewalk or street, a permit is usually required. Call us and we'll explain what your project needs.

How long is the rental period?

As long as your project requires. We'll confirm the rental period in your quote.

Are you licensed and placehold?

Yes. RIAA Scaffolding Services is a licensed and placehold contractor.

Who inspects the scaffolding?

Our team inspects the scaffolding after installation and before it's dismantled.
